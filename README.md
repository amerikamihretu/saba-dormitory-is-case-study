# Assessment of Dormitory Problem Reporting and Follow-Up: An Information Systems Case Study of Saba Male Dormitory at Addis Ababa University, Arat Kilo Campus

## 1. Project Description

This research project investigates the existing dormitory maintenance problem reporting and follow-up processes at **Saba Male Dormitory, Addis Ababa University, Arat Kilo Campus**, from an Information Systems perspective.

The study examines how facility and maintenance problems are reported by students, recorded and referred by the dormitory proctor, and handled by the campus maintenance office. It aims to identify operational challenges, communication gaps, record-keeping practices, and difficulties in tracking maintenance requests until resolution.

Using a qualitative single-site descriptive case study design, the research will collect information through semi-structured interviews, non-participant observation, and conditional document review. The collected data will be analyzed using thematic analysis following Braun and Clarke's (2006) six-phase framework.

The study aims to provide an evidence-based understanding of the existing reporting and follow-up workflow and identify practical recommendations for improving information flow, communication, and accountability.

## 2. Research Objectives

### General Objective

To assess the facility-problem reporting and follow-up process at Saba Male Dormitory, Addis Ababa University, Arat Kilo Campus.

### Specific Objectives

- To describe and examine the existing procedures used to report, record, refer, and follow up on facility and maintenance problems.
- To identify the reporting and follow-up challenges experienced by students, the dormitory proctor, and maintenance office staff, and document their suggested improvements.

## 3. Research Methodology

- **Research Approach:** Qualitative research approach.
- **Research Design:** Single-site descriptive case study.
- **Study Area:** Saba Male Dormitory, Addis Ababa University, Arat Kilo Campus.
- **Sampling Technique:** Purposive sampling.
- **Intended Participants:** Six participants consisting of four resident students, one dormitory proctor, and one campus maintenance office staff member.

### Data Collection Methods

1. **Semi-Structured Interviews:** Interviews with resident students, the dormitory proctor, and campus maintenance office staff.
2. **Non-Participant Observation:** Observation of administrative reporting activities at the dormitory proctor's office.
3. **Conditional Document Review:** Examination of sample entries from the dormitory complaint logbook, subject to formal administrative authorization.

### Data Analysis

The collected qualitative materials will be analyzed using thematic analysis based on Braun and Clarke's (2006) six-phase framework:

1. Data familiarization.
2. Initial code generation.
3. Searching for themes.
4. Reviewing and refining themes.
5. Defining and naming themes.
6. Producing the synthesis.

## 7. Research Ethics and Data Management

The research will follow academic ethical standards throughout its implementation.

- Participation in interviews will be voluntary.
- Informed consent will be obtained before conducting interviews.
- Participant identities will be protected through anonymization.
- Personal identifying information will not be included in shared research datasets.
- Access to administrative complaint records will depend on formal authorization.
- Research materials will be stored securely and accessed only by authorized group members.

Only cleaned and anonymized research materials will be included in the repository where appropriate. Confidential recordings and documents containing personal identifying information will not be publicly uploaded.
